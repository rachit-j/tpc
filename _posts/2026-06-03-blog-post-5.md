**What is one plan to express yourself through writing in the short term?**

To express myself through writing in the short term, I'm planning on trying to be less critical of myself in my writing and trying to assimilate into what I consider "normal" writing. Many times when I write, I'm overly cautious and I do my best to "write as a normal human would". After taking this class, I feel live I've gotten more intentional with my tone and language, so whenever I write, my plan is to keep the tone in which I originally brainstorm my ideas. I'm planning on journaling this Summer, as one of my overall goals has been to become more physically fit and mentally stronger, and journaling my raw thoughts would be great practice to express myself through writing. Maybe at the end, I'll show it to a few close friends so I can reflect how my writing creates impact.

**An answer to the question: What is one bold, crazy idea you can think of with regard to your writing in the long term?**

In the long term, I want to focus a lot on writing with different fonts, sort of interjecting them in my sentences to emphasize and give more context/tone to specific words or phrases. While this is super uncommon and may look "weird", I want to try and incorporate some changing fonts to my writing. After all, the only font I like to write in right now is Calibri, but maybe I'll find a good set of fonts that I can incorporate in my sentences, and give them more meaning then plain text.
