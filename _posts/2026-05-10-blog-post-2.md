**How did teamwork go for the presentation? How do you see yourself in team writing tasks?**

For the presentation, teamwork went very well. I was able to meet with my partner Alice mutliple times and although it took a bit to settle on a company, once we were able to do that, the presentation was relatively easy with both of us working together in the same location (which proves why in my opinion working in person is better than working remotely). Since we met in person, it was a lot easier to get the presentation done faster and analyze Anthropic's values, and we split up the work and speaking portions very evenly.

In future team writing tasks, I see myself mostly helping guide decisionmaking and keeping track of deadlines; that's what I'm used to doing and what my skills are in people management. While I also contribute my fair share of work, I like to make sure that for future team writing tasks, everyone's voices and **inputs** are also heard and accounted for. I want to be more of someone who gently corrects my team's writing towards its strongest goal, not controlling, but guiding.
